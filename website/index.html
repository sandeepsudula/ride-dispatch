How it works

No new app to convince your friends to download. It works with the ride groups people already use.

Riders ask as usual

"Need a ride SM to Austin at 5pm." Ride Dispatch reads requests from your group chat: the place, the time, how many people.

It groups them

People going the same way at about the same time go into one car, including pickups and drop-offs along the route.

The driver decides

Once a trip is full enough to be worth it, the driver gets one alert with every rider's name, number and time. Go, or don't.

Two sides of the same trip

The car is already going. Ride Dispatch just makes sure it isn't going empty.

For drivers

Fill your empty seats

  • Set how many seats you have and the minimum riders that make a trip worth it
  • Get one alert per trip, not a stream of messages
  • Riders on your way, both directions, grouped in pickup order
  • You never post anything unless you choose to
For riders

Get a ride that's already leaving

  • Ask in your group the way you already do
  • Get matched with a driver heading your way at your time
  • Ride with people from your own community
  • Round trips: say when you're coming back

Who it's for

Started at Texas State, for the trips people in our community make every week.

Students

Getting between campus, Austin, the airport and home without a car of your own.

Commuters

Working professionals on the San Marcos–Austin run who'd rather not drive it alone.

Drivers going home anyway

Already on the road, happy to share the cost and the company.
